The mission

Among Us, minus the screens

You walk into a hall as a crewmate or an impostor. You do tasks with your hands, lie with your face and vote with your finger. Nobody needs a phone and nobody needs experience.

How a round works

Four phases, one big mess

Each round runs about 15 minutes. Everyone moves around the hall at the same time, so the lying happens face to face.

  1. Phase 1

    Role cards

    You get a folded card. Open it in secret. One player in every five is an impostor and the rest are crew.

  2. Phase 2

    Tasks

    Crew visit stations around the hall and a volunteer stamps each finished task. Impostors carry a fake card and look busy.

  3. Phase 3

    Bodies and meetings

    Find a body and shout. Everyone freezes, talks for 60 seconds, then points at a suspect together on a count of three.

  4. Phase 4

    Verdict

    Crew win when the wall bar of task stamps is full or every impostor is out. Impostors win when they match the crew in numbers.

How to kill

The Wink

An impostor locks eyes with a crewmate and winks. Five seconds later that crewmate dies as dramatically as they can, then lies where they fell. The best death scenes get applause.

Sabotage

Reactor Meltdown

A volunteer rings a bell. Two crewmates must reach two marked spots on opposite sides of the hall and hold them for 10 seconds, or the impostors win on the spot.

Sabotage

Comms Down

For 45 seconds nobody may speak. Everything is gestures, pointing and very bad charades. Impostors love this part.

After you die

Ghost mode

Dead players become silent ghosts. You can still finish tasks for the crew, so every stamp you earn counts. Nobody sits out.

The tasks

Six stations. Zero dignity.

Every task is short, silly and watched by whoever is standing nearby. Finish one and a volunteer stamps your card. Try it below: stamp each task and fill the wall bar.

Impostors can only win if the crew stalls. Keep stamping.

  • 25 sec

    Fix Wiring

    A knot of colored cords, four sockets and a volunteer with a sand timer. Match every cord to its socket before the sand runs out.

    Looks easy until three people are watching you and one of them is lying.

    Props: cords, socket board, sand timer

    Done
  • 30 sec

    Divert Power

    Five big switches and a card that shows the right order for three seconds. Flip them from memory and the lamp on top lights up.

    Your friends will shout the wrong order at you. Maybe on purpose.

    Props: switch board, lamp, order cards

    Done
  • 30 sec

    Clear Asteroids

    Stand behind the line and land paper-ball asteroids in the target rings. Five hits clears the field and earns your stamp.

    Every miss gets a loud "ooooh" from the whole room.

    Props: paper balls, floor targets

    Done
  • 25 sec

    Start Reactor

    Watch a five-pad light pattern, then copy it exactly. One wrong pad and the reactor resets. You can play a mini version below.

    Silence is rule one, so everyone watches you sweat.

    Props: light pads, volunteer

    Done
  • 30 sec

    Clean O2 Filter

    Sort a pile of colored leaves into matching cups using only your non-writing hand while the volunteer keeps shuffling the cups.

    Peak concentration face, guaranteed. People will photograph it.

    Props: paper leaves, colored cups

    Done
  • 20 sec

    Stabilize Steering

    Guide a metal loop along a twisting wire without touching it. A buzzer screams on every touch so the whole hall knows.

    The shakiest hands in the room will be exposed. Imposters have no advantage here.

    Props: wire course, loop, buzzer

    Done

Ship rules

Six rules that keep it fun

  • Winks only

    The wink is the only way to eliminate someone. No touching, no tagging, no grabbing.

  • No running

    Move fast but keep your feet under control. Nobody wins by knocking into a friend.

  • Bodies stay put

    If you die, stay where you fell until someone reports you. Then you become a ghost.

  • Ghosts stay silent

    Ghosts can do tasks but cannot speak, point or hint. Keep what you saw to yourself.

  • Accuse the play

    Everyone is lying on purpose. Keep it friendly and laugh when you get caught.

  • Volunteers decide

    If something is unclear, the nearest volunteer makes the call and the game goes on.

Before you come

Questions players ask

Do I need to know Among Us?

No. If you know the game, you have a head start. If you do not, a volunteer explains everything in two minutes and you learn by playing.

When and where is it?

It happens at IIIT Bhopal. The date and exact venue will be announced on the Spark Instagram and LinkedIn pages, so follow both and register now.

Who can register?

The form asks for your roll number, so register with your IIIT Bhopal roll number. If you have a question about eligibility, message the Spark team on Instagram.

What should I wear?

Something comfortable that you can fall dramatically in, plus closed shoes. Skip masks and costumes that hide your face, because winks need to be seen.

Can I come with friends?

Yes, but everyone registers separately. Roles are random, so you might end up trying to catch your best friend.

What if I get eliminated early?

You become a ghost. You still do tasks and help the crew win, you just cannot talk. Nobody sits out.

Can I change my details after registering?

Yes. Open this page on the same device and use Edit details on your pass. Your code stays the same.
